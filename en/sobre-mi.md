## ⚙️ SoftGnom Philosophy & Architecture

**SoftGnom** originated as an independent software engineering space with a clear premise: **bringing efficient computing and artificial intelligence directly to the local machine**, eliminating unnecessary dependencies on cloud services and prioritizing total control over data and infrastructure.

### Design Principles
- **Sovereignty and Privacy (*Offline-First*):** Software must be capable of operating autonomously, securely, and privately in local or isolated environments.
- **Modularity and Decoupling:** A strict separation between processing logic, data management, and the visualization layer, allowing each component to scale independently or integrate with various technologies.
- **Technological Pragmatism:** Using the optimal tool for each specific problem, combining the performance of low-level code with the agility of modern automation and containerization.

---

## 🧰 Core Technology Ecosystem

The tools, languages, and environments that form the foundation of this laboratory include:

- **Programming Languages:** Python, C++, C, Java, Dart, JavaScript, Shell/Bash.
- **Architecture, Data & Backend:** Modular design, REST APIs, SQL (PostgreSQL, Oracle SQL), NoSQL (MongoDB, Redis).
- **Infrastructure, Virtualization & Containers:** Linux, Docker, Podman, Proxmox, local network and service administration, AWS. - **Local AI & Data:** Orchestration of local LLMs (Ollama, development agents), data processing (Pandas, Scikit-learn, XGBoost), and accelerated computing (CUDA).
- **Interfaces & Prototyping:** HTML5/CSS3, Flutter, Jekyll.
